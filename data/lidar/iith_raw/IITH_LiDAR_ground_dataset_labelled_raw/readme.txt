This dataset has LiDAR points clouds with ground and non-ground points. 
The raw_data folder contains the raw point cloud.
labelled_data foler contains the labelled point cloud with ground truth. The point clouds are in the form of pcd files with XYZRGB format. Ground points are red (r=255,g=0,b=0). Non-ground points are green (r=0, g=255, b=0).
